Harmonize a melody

Click cells to write a melody (one note per beat, starting at beat 1). The hidden states are chords; the notes you wrote are the observations. The Viterbi algorithm finds the most probable chord sequence under an HMM trained on Bach chorales.

Which style is this melody?

One HMM per style (Bach chorales, Palestrina, folk tunes) scores the melody you wrote on the Harmonize tab using the forward algorithm with scaling factors. The style whose model finds the melody most probable wins. Mark notes as short (eighth notes) in the row under the grid.

Tempo tracking with a Kalman filter

Humans tap with timing jitter. A linear dynamical system tracks the hidden tempo (period and its drift) from noisy inter-tap intervals. Tap steadily, then speed up or slow down.
